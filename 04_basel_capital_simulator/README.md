# Case Study 04 — Basel III Capital Requirement Simulator

## Overview

Interactive simulator for computing Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA) and minimum capital requirements under the Basel III Standardized Approach (SA), calibrated to SBS Ecuador regulatory norms.

## Status

📋 **Planned**

## Scope

- Credit risk RWA calculation under Standardized Approach
- Tier 1 and Total Capital Ratio computation
- Sensitivity analysis: impact of portfolio mix on capital adequacy
- Comparison: SA vs. simplified IRB estimates

## References

- Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (2017). *Basel III: Finalising post-crisis reforms.* BIS.
- SBS Ecuador (2023). *Normas para la gestión del riesgo de crédito.* Resolución No. SB-2023-0432.
